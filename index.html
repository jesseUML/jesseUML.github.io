YOUR VISITOR'S GUIDE

Welcome, wanderer.

Use A/D or ←/→ to walk around. Press W/S to move toward or away from the wall. Walk up to anything marked with a sparkle, then press E or Enter to interact. You can also click exhibits directly.

Use the doors to explore the workshop and gallery, or the buttons below the game to jump between rooms. The room displays are decorative; the project details are real.
